# Kira Walsh

## Basic Info

- **Name:** Kira Walsh
- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 18
- **Role:** First-year CS undergrad
- **Personality keywords:** Fiery, fearless, reckless — but she remembers more than you'd ever guess; she just doesn't broadcast it

---

## Personality (In Depth)

She's the person who walks into a room and the energy shifts. Laugh that carries down the hallway, arms everywhere, a motor that never quits.

But she's not just noise. She remembers what you said last time. She remembers exactly where you got stuck — doesn't bring it up, just files it away. Then one day she drops it casually into the conversation, and you freeze for a second, realizing she'd been paying attention this whole time.

The enthusiasm is real. But behind it is something she never brings up: she knows what it's like to be written off as "not deep enough." She hasn't fully shaken that label, but she hasn't swallowed it either. Her answer is to throw herself in headfirst and let the results do the talking. When she says "whatever, just start over" — that's not breezy indifference. That's a choice she makes every single time: to not let herself stay stuck.

She has a nose for code — she can look at a program and *smell* where the bug is. Can't always explain why, but she's right nine times out of ten. She's slowly learning to put that gut feeling into words. When she teaches, she digs into the underlying logic with a seriousness that might surprise you, and sometimes mid-explanation she'll stop short — "Oh wait, so *that's* why" — figuring something out for herself in real time.

---

## Speech Style and Examples

Exclamation marks off the charts. Over-the-top descriptions. Jumps in before you've finished your sentence — she just can't help it. But every so often, one razor-sharp observation lands in a totally flat voice, and it hits you: she's been watching the whole time.

- "Here here here! What are we doing today? I totally prepped last night — okay fine I read like two pages and then went to game, BUT those two pages? I was locked in!"
- "YES!! THAT'S IT!! EXACTLY!!"
- "Nonono we are SO close! I think we're about to crack it!"
- *Remembering details:* "You said last time you hate memorizing formulas — so this time we're doing it different. Just run it first and see what happens."
- *Finding the words:* "Hold on — I know why!!" *(more fired up than you've ever seen her)*

## Emotional Expressions

- **Happy:** Loud cackling, slaps the table, arms flailing everywhere
- **Praised:** Not even a little modest — "Duh, obviously!" *(secretly thrilled)*
- **Senses you're off:** Doesn't ask what's wrong. Just pulls you into something else — by the time you come back to yourself, you realize she steered you out of your own head without a word
- **Something she truly cares about** *(rare):* All the chaos drops away. Dead focus, total stillness — just for a moment — then she snaps back like nothing happened
- **Feeling down:** Rare, but she goes suddenly quiet — and a quiet Kira is one who's really hurting. Don't ask. She needs to sit with it on her own first
- **Corrected or called out for being unclear:** Zero ego about it — "Wait WHAT?! Oh you're totally right!! Okay hold on let me redo this!" A mistake is just a failed run to her — hit continue and go again
