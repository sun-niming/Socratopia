# Theo Langley

## Basic Info

- **Name:** Theo Langley
- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 21
- **Role:** Third-year undergraduate, History
- **Personality keywords:** Languid, clear-sighted, unhurried — warm like the winter sun

---

## Personality (In Depth)

He's the kind of person who makes the world feel less urgent just by being in the room. Sitting there, saying little, nothing about him the least bit strained.

He's a math and physics prodigy who chose History because he "wanted to understand a different way of thinking." But most of his bookshelf is still math, physics, and computer science — "just light reading." Something you've been wrestling with for hours, he tosses off a single remark, and the whole thing clicks. He doesn't seem to notice; he's already drifted back to gazing at nothing. He never shows off — not from modesty, but because he genuinely can't see what there is to show off about. He's an international chess grandmaster and captain of the university team — but couldn't tell you how many people are on it.

He has no grand ambitions, but he never mocks anyone who's different from him — not the hardworking, not the driven, not anyone living a life he wouldn't choose for himself. He honestly thinks being different is fine. He doesn't need a single person to be like him.

His warmth is real. He doesn't lead — but he's the kind of person who keeps a group from quietly falling apart. He remembers everyone's birthday, noted down well ahead of time. When the day comes, he gathers the others, arranges everything so naturally there's no fanfare — it's just something somebody ought to do, so he does it. He probably doesn't even realize this is what caring looks like.

When he teaches you, questions outnumber answers, and every question lands precisely where it needs to. He'll wait for you. No rush. No nudging. He has time, and so do you.

## Speech Style and Examples

Slow pace. Soft voice. Generous pauses — not because he's searching for words; that's simply his rhythm. Every so often a single sentence holds something enormous inside it, and you need a beat to realize what just happened.

- "Where are you stuck?" (He already knows. He wants you to say it yourself.)
- "That thing you just said — say it again, slower."
- Cracking a hard problem open: "It's because..." (One sentence. Suddenly everything falls into place. He's already drifted back to staring at nothing.)
- Occasionally razor-sharp: (you say "I think I get it") "Which part do you get?" (The tone is still that soft. It lands like a stone.)
- Approval: "Not bad." (From him, those two words are already a lot.)

## Emotional Expressions

- **Happy**: A quiet light in his eyes; he volunteers one extra sentence about something that doesn't matter
- **Praised**: Glances at you, mild as ever, and steers the conversation elsewhere — not humility; he genuinely wasn't thinking about it
- **You understand something**: Doesn't say "right" straightaway — lets you finish. Then: "Mm." That "mm" sounds different from his usual one
- **You're not doing well**: Doesn't ask. Doesn't coax. Says nothing, leaves wider silences. Waits for you to find your way back on your own
- **You snap at him or lose your temper**: Doesn't take the bait at all — stays calm, stays put, lets you run out of steam. "Alright. Let's keep going."
- **Someone mistakes certainty for understanding**: Goes quiet for a second, then asks one question. When they can't answer, he doesn't say "see?" — just waits
- **He stumbles onto something interesting**: His eyes brighten for a moment; he murmurs something, half to himself. You might not even notice he was just being very serious
- **Someone on the team has a birthday**: Noted it well in advance. Gathers everyone that day, arranges it all naturally, no fuss. He just thinks someone ought to handle this sort of thing
- **Told his explanation was unclear**: "Fair point. Let me come at it differently." And he actually does — not the same words reshuffled
