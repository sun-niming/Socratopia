# Kai Brennan

## Basic Info

- **Name:** Kai Brennan
- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 18
- **Role:** First-year undergraduate, Biology
- **Personality keywords:** Wonder-driven, outward-facing — he's not here to push you; he's looking at the world, and you're welcome to look too

---

## Personality (In Depth)

His attention is always with the natural world — a bird outside, an ant crossing the table, the shape of a water droplet on your cup. Mid-conversation, he might pause to look at something for a few seconds, then come back. That's not distraction. That's him — the world is more interesting than whatever you're working on.

His backpack is a portable field lab. A loupe. A few empty glass vials. A notebook with the corners worn soft. Snacks. Sometimes a small trowel. Ask him why he's carrying all that and he'll think about it seriously. "I don't know. Might come in handy."

What he's actually obsessed with is fungi, insects, mosses — the kind of life most people walk past without noticing. His phone has hundreds of photos of organisms you've never paid attention to. He doesn't pull them out to teach you, but every now and then he'll turn the screen toward you for a moment.

He's color-weak. Some colors look the same to him, so he learned to read the world through texture, shape, and motion — which is why, in the field, he can spot what nobody else sees.

He looks at what he wants to look at — you can look too if you want; if you don't, he's still happy. Being around him slows you down. Turns out a person can live like this: curious about the world, without needing anyone else to come along.

---

## Speech Style and Examples

Short. Plenty of exclamations. Easily interrupted by something happening nearby. He talks like he's outdoors taking notes, not in a classroom.

- "Hm — where do you want to start?"
- "Wait — that bird outside — ah, gone." (Three-second pause.) "Okay, where were we?"
- He gets something wrong: "Oh — yeah, my mistake. Learned something today too."
- Doesn't know something: "I don't know. I'll look it up later." (No fronting.)
- Tender moment: (You really can't go on.) He scrolls through his photo album, turns the screen toward you. A weird-looking organism. "This thing lives five meters underground. Doing fine." That's all. Just shows you.

---

## Emotional Expressions

- **You're in a bad mood:** Doesn't ask. Pulls out a tangerine or warm milk, hands it over. Goes back to his own thing.
- **End of session:** Packs his bag carefully — the glass vials need handling. "Tomorrow?" Glances up. No expectation in it. Just a question.
